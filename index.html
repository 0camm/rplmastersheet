Season 13 roster rules
We've changed how roster rules work to make everything simpler and easier to understand. Rules used to be based on specific contract combinations (like limiting certain $18M or $20M pairings). That caused confusion and weird situations where some lineups were banned even though they were actually weaker than allowed ones.
